Learn safer digital habits

Lesson 1

Pause under pressure

Unexpected threats and deadlines can pressure you into acting quickly. Verify the request independently.

Lesson 2

Question surprise rewards

Free prizes or game currency can be bait for private information. Ask someone you trust before continuing.

Lesson 3

Protect private codes

Do not share OTPs, PINs, or passwords with someone who contacts you asking for them.

Try a scenario

An unexpected message says: “Your account will close in 10 minutes. Send your OTP.”
